How I will address the issue of car-centric urban planning

In order to address the issue of car-centric urban planning, I propose a number of changes to the way cities are designed. First, we need to prioritize pedestrians and cyclists over cars. This means creating more walkable and bikeable streets, with dedicated lanes for each mode of transportation. We also need to invest in public transportation, making it more accessible and affordable for everyone. Finally, we need to rethink the way we use our public spaces, creating more parks and green spaces that encourage people to spend time outside and connect with their communities.

And how I will implement these changes is by working with local government officials to advocate for policy changes that support sustainable transportation options and by engaging with the community to build support for these initiatives.
